    What happens every evening

    1. 6 PM: the latest readings come in

      Today's PM2.5 from CPCB and DPCC monitors, tomorrow's weather forecast, and NASA satellite counts of crop fires in Punjab and Haryana.

    2. The model forecasts school hours

      It predicts the average PM2.5 between 7 AM and 2 PM tomorrow for each area. Calm nights and low winds trap smoke until morning, and the model has learned that from two years of data.

    3. You get one plain message

      The level, what to move indoors, and why the air is bad. Runs on AWS Lambda, EventBridge, S3 and SNS.

    Air data: CPCB and DPCC monitors via OpenAQ. Weather and CAMS: Open-Meteo. Fires: NASA FIRMS.
